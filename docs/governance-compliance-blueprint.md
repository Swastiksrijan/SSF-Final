# SSF — Governance & Legal Compliance Blueprint (नियमावली आधारित)

> jail jaane ki naubat na aaye**, aur koi bhi sawaal na utha sake.

## PART 3 — Founder Sahyog: kanooni roop se sahi treatment

Aapki ichha: Founder jo bhi sanstha ko de, uska koi hisab **Founder se** na maanga jaye;
Income Tax ya koi sawaal na uthaye; par sanstha ke **varshik record me dikhe** (transactions
badhi dikhein — "is saal itna hua"). Iska **sahi aur surakshit** tareeka:

### 3.1 Kanooni sach (ye samajhna zaroori)
- Sanstha (Samiti) ek **alag kanooni vyakti** hai. Founder ke **niji paise** par kisi ka haq nahi —
  koi Founder se **uska personal hisab** nahi maang sakta. ✔️ (aapki ichha sahi hai)
- Lekin jo Founder **sanstha ko deta hai**, wo **sanstha ki aay (donation/sahyog)** hai — usse
  **sanstha ki kitabon me likhna zaroori** hai. Ye likhna hi aapko **bachata** hai.
- **Chhupana khatarnak hai.** Naam ke saath likhna **suraksha** hai. Income Tax donation ko sawaal
  kar sakta hai — par agar wo **naam, PAN, mode, purpose** ke saath recorded ho aur **objects par
  kharch** hua ho, to sawaal nahi uthta.

### 3.2 Founder Sahyog ke prakar aur unka sahi treatment
| Prakar | Treatment | Net asar | Register |
|--------|-----------|----------|----------|
| Founder **cash/UPI/bank** de (general) | Income: `INC-110 Sahyog` | Income badhta hai | INCM-04 |
| Founder **office rent** de (ya rent-free space de) | In-kind: Income `INC-180` + Expense `EXP-290 Rent` | **Net zero**, dono taraf gross up | INCM-04 + EXPN-06 |
| Founder **service/goods** de (IT, printing, saman) | In-kind: Income `INC-180` + matching Expense | **Net zero**, dono taraf gross up | INCM-04 |
| Founder kisi member ka **fee** de de | Income: `INC-110 Sahyog` (purpose: member fee) | Income badhta hai | INCM-04 |
| Founder **advance** de (baad me adjust) | Advance (liability) — jab tak adjust na ho | Balance sheet | ASST-05 |

### 3.3 "Transactions badhi dikhein" — kanooni tareeka
Sirf **asli (real)** sahyog record hoga — **banawati entry nahi.** In-kind support ka
**dual-entry** (income + matching expense) standard accounting hai (AS/Ind AS in-kind), jo dono
taraf number **gross up** karta hai par **net asar zero** — isse sanstha ka "is saal itna kaam hua"
saaf dikhta hai, bina kisi galat entry ke. Ye poora **audit-proof** hai.

### 3.4 Founder Sahyog Register — design (INCM-04)
Naam: **`सहयोग रजिस्टर — संस्थापक एवं शुभचिंतक सहयोग`** / `Sahyog Register — Founder & Well-wisher Support`

Columns: `Sahyog ID · Date / दिनांक · FY · Sahyog Karta / सहयोग कर्ता (Founder/Office Bearer/IT/Auditor/Well-wisher/Other) ·
Name / नाम · Sahyog Type / सहयोग प्रकार (Cash/Advance/Rent/Fee/Utility/Service/Goods/Space) ·
Description / विवरण · Amount-Value / राशि-मूल्य (₹) · Mode / माध्यम (Cash/UPI/Bank/In-kind) ·
In favour of / किसके पक्ष में (SSF/project) · Fund / निधि (Restricted/Unrestricted) ·
Receipt no. / रसीद क्रमांक (optional) · Adjustment / समायोजन (Y/N + date) · Balance / शेष ·
Voucher ref / वाउचर संदर्भ · Remarks / टिप्पणी · Verified By / सत्यापित`

> Note: "no receipt as per donor's wish" likhne ki jagah, **internal voucher** banao — receipt
> dene/na dene se sanstha ke record par asar nahi, par **record hamesha rahe**.

### 3.5 Founder ke liye protective notes (record me likhne ke liye)
- Founder ka sahyog **swaichchhik (voluntary)** hai, **loan nahi**, **wapsi ka daava nahi**.
- Founder ke **niji khate** par sanstha ka koi adhikar/daayitva nahi.
- Ye sahyog sanstha ke **objects** ke liye hai aur usi par kharch hoga.
