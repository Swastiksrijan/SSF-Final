Donation & Refund Policy

Swastik Srijan Foundation — Donation, Cancellation & Refund Policy

1. General Terms

Swastik Srijan Foundation is committed to transparency and to ensuring that all donations are used efficiently for rural empowerment, education, and social development projects. By donating to Swastik Srijan Foundation you accept the terms described below.

2. Donation Exemption & Receipts

  • Donations may be eligible for tax benefits under Section 80G / 12AB of the Income Tax Act (as applicable). Eligibility depends on the donor and the applicable law.
  • Donors will receive an official donation receipt by email after payment verification.
  • Please provide an accurate PAN (when required) and contact details at the time of donation to generate a valid tax-exemption certificate.

3. Refund & Cancellation Policy

Non-Refundable: As a registered non-profit organisation, donations are ordinarily non-refundable because funds are allocated to active programs immediately after processing.

Exceptional Refunds: A refund may be considered only in the following exceptional cases:

  • Technical error causing a duplicate deduction (the same amount debited twice for one donation).
  • An unauthorized or fraudulent transaction made using your payment method.

Refund Request Timeline: For duplicate debits or technical errors, donors must notify us in writing within 7 days of the transaction date.

4. How to Request a Refund

If you believe a refund is warranted due to a technical error or duplicate charge, please contact us with proof of the transaction:

Email: info@swastiksrijan.in

Phone: +91-9718346691

Required details: Full Name, Transaction ID, Payment Date, Amount, and Proof of Payment (bank statement or payment screenshot).

After verification, eligible refunds will be processed to the original payment method within 7–10 working days.
